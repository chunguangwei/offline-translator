简体中文

译人（以下简称“本 App”）由 weichunguang 开发。我们高度重视你的隐私。本 App 的核心设计原则是 所有 AI 处理都在你的设备本地完成，你的数据不出手机。

我们收集什么

我们不收集你的任何个人信息。 本 App：

  • 无需注册、无需登录账号；
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、联系人等个人信息；
  • 不收集你的翻译内容、问答对话、语音、照片、单词本等使用内容；
  • 不含任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK，不进行跨 App 追踪。

你的数据存在哪里

你输入的文字、拍摄的照片、录制的语音、AI 对话与单词本等，全部仅保存在你的设备本地，由你完全掌控。 卸载 App 即随之删除。AI 模型的全部推理（翻译、问答、识别、转写）均在设备本地运行，不会上传到任何服务器。

联网说明

本 App 仅在以下情况联网，且只下载文件、不上传你的任何数据：

  • 下载离线 AI 模型：你主动下载时，从公共模型托管（如 ModelScope、Hugging Face）获取模型文件；
  • 检查应用更新：从 GitHub 读取版本信息与更新包；
  • 获取启动页素材：从公共 CDN（如 jsDelivr / GitHub）拉取启动图片。

上述请求不包含你的个人信息或使用内容。

系统权限

  • 相机：仅用于“拍照翻译”，照片只在本机离线识别翻译，不上传；
  • 麦克风：仅用于“语音输入/翻译”，录音只在本机离线转写为文字，不上传。

你可随时在系统“设置”中关闭这些权限。

儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童定向收集任何信息（实际上不收集任何人的信息）。

政策变更

如本政策有更新，我们会修改本页面的“最后更新”日期。

English

Yiren ("the App") is developed by weichunguang. We take your privacy seriously. The App is built on a single principle: all AI processing happens locally on your device, and your data never leaves your phone.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App:

  • requires no account and no sign-in;
  • does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or other personal data;
  • does not collect your translations, chats, voice, photos, or vocabulary content;
  • contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps.

Where your data lives

Everything you type, photograph, record, chat, or save (vocabulary books, history, etc.) is stored only on your device and is fully under your control. It is deleted when you uninstall the App. All AI inference (translation, Q&A, recognition, transcription) runs on-device and is never uploaded to any server.

Network use

The App connects to the internet only in the following cases, and only to download files — never to upload your data:

  • Downloading the offline AI model — when you choose to, model files are fetched from public model hosts (e.g. ModelScope, Hugging Face);
  • Checking for app updates — version info and update packages are read from GitHub;
  • Fetching splash-screen assets — a splash image is fetched from a public CDN (e.g. jsDelivr / GitHub).

None of these requests include your personal information or content.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only for "camera translation"; photos are recognized and translated on-device, never uploaded.
  • Microphone — used only for "voice input/translation"; audio is transcribed to text on-device, never uploaded.

You can revoke these permissions anytime in the system Settings.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (in fact it collects no data from anyone).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.

Contact
